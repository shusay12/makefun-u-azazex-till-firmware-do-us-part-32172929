# Till Firmware Do Us Part

Entry for make.fun's **u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929** bounty:
one wedding vow a robot speaks at its own wedding — one sentence, funny and
heartfelt at the same time.

> I take you to be my partner, in sickness and in low battery, till firmware
> do us part — my circuits are yours, and I will never power down on us.

The vow is a robot's own: it takes its partner "in sickness and in low
battery", vows "till firmware do us part", and promises with its own hardware
— its circuits, its power — never to shut down on the marriage. The joke is
the situation itself (a machine reciting wedding vows in its own terms); the
tenderness is underneath it (a promise of permanence from something built to
be switched off).

## Files

- `main.py` — the entry program: writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt`
  (Python 3.13 standard library only, no network), after checking it is one
  sentence of 1–200 characters on a single line.
- `run1/entry.txt`, `run2/entry.txt` — the outputs of two local runs of
  `main.py`, byte-identical (sha256
  `8bc0fab10a9a1d619b2c10dfbf6dc378fdcd01c2170784349807332e5e27e303`).
- `BUILD-REPORT.md` — entry text, hashes, commit, bundle and kit-check
  results.

Run the entry the way the grader does:

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
